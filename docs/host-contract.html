The host contract

What an application (the host) supplies, and what it must not rebuild because the library does it. A host that keeps to this writes reading and rendering only. The jobs themselves are in capabilities; a worked start is the quickstart.

In the terms of the glossary: the host chooses the world schemas (its own or published w.*), loads what it needs of the knowledge worlds (k.*), and keeps one live world per session in a World object. It reads text into requests and renders replies and views; the live world does the rest. Where the host's own code belongs (an effect, not the world) is on kinds of worlds.

Where this agrees with PSys2's host boundary, and where not

PSys2 wrote docs/host-boundary.md from its side (at its main 9e370c47). The split is the same: reading, translation, the user interface, its server, its own worlds' content and its evaluation stay in the host; mechanisms go to the library, facts to sop-knowledge-worlds. Where the two differ, this wording is the interface:

  • Names. It names std.things, std.dialogue, std.space, std.knowledge, std.route. Here: shared things and views are part of std.schema / std.acts (show); the dialogue is std.focus with turn; space is std.relations with std.boxes; knowledge loading is createCatalog (a function, not a module of rules); routing is route.
  • An English pack of the acts. It plans the acts' English forms as "its English pack". The library ships no word lists: words for acts come from knowledge (k.actions) or the host. actReply writes plain English as a default only.
  • The compute budget. It lists its per-turn tick budget as general. The library bounds each of its own calls ({ budget }, world.stats()); a budget over a whole turn that includes host code (reading, rendering) stays the host's guard.
  • Legends and the interpreter. It keeps its legend loader and interpreter "until the library covers it". The library's World is the interpreter; PSys2's legend dialect (core.key, core.operation) is mapped by compat/psys2/mapping.sop; a host keeps only the mapping, not a second interpreter.
  • The card generator and coverage grid it lists as "lib when agreed": they are here (schemaCard, schemaGrid) for worlds declared with std.schema; its cards of other worlds (rule worlds) stay generated by it until those worlds declare a schema.
