# The 22 gaps of the two replays, reduced to five general mechanisms

*Reviewer, 2026-11-16. The owner found 22 mechanisms too many and asked whether they are particular cases of a few general ones. I think they are. This is a proposal for the author to test, counter or improve; the letters refer to `replay-owner-2026-11-16.md` (A–L) and `replay-owner-2026-11-14-15.md` (M–V).*

*Correction from the owner on gap A: only English is part of the core; translation is the application's business and not the book's. What remains of A is tolerance to misspelt and non-standard ENGLISH words, and it falls under mechanism 1 below.*

## 1. One rule for finding what was named: the nearest match within a tolerance

The book already has this rule for values: a word names a region, the point asked for is compared with what can be realised, the nearest within the tolerance is taken and said, a tie is asked, beyond the tolerance it is refused (4.4, 6.5). The proposal is that the SAME rule finds everything else a message names. A reference is a description (a kind, qualities, relations, an order, a source, a time); the candidates are scored by distance on each part of the description.

| What is being found | Candidates and distance | Gaps it covers |
|---|---|---|
| a thing | the things in the state; distance in the kind tree, in the quality spaces, in place | B ("lake" finds the water) |
| a thing by its source | the same things; the dimension is provenance (asked by the user / assumed by the system) | R ("the extra ones") |
| a past value of a thing | the states of the thing in the journal; the dimension is time | O ("where it was") |
| a member by order | the members of a whole; the dimension is the whole's default order | N |
| a part of a thing | its parts, with the part that supports or contains preferred for relations of support and containment | H ("on the swing" → the seat) |
| a part that is expected and absent | the parts in the graph of a thing in focus; found → it may be made | S ("the fish" of the pond); this is R37 seen from the other side |
| the sense of a word | the senses listed for it; the cost of each given the two kinds | L ("over") |
| a word itself | the vocabulary plus the names of things in the instance; distance between strings | what is left of A ("watter", "th epond", "upper") |
| a realisation | the kinds that have a picture, by distance in the kind tree; then composition from parts | G (the swing, the grandfather) |

One table in the book — what is searched, among what, with which distance, and the single outcome rule (one within tolerance: take and say; several: ask; none but expected or indefinite: make; none: refuse and offer the nearest) — replaces nine separate mechanisms.

## 2. Whatever has values is a space, the kind included; a value may be a point or a region

Two extensions of chapter 4 as it stands.

The kind of a thing is the value of a slot (`key=kind means=kind`), and the kind tree is its space, with a distance (mechanism 1 uses it). Then "become a boy", "make the avatar a robot" is a change of a quality — the third basic change — on that slot. No fifth basic change is needed (gap I). What must be said is which other qualities survive: those whose slots the new kind also has.

A value may be a region, not only a point. A meeting already is one (an interval on the time axis). So is "5–10 nodes" (an interval on the quantity axis), and so are water, ground, sky, grass: things whose place is a region of the page (gap C). The layer (what is in front of what) is one more axis of the page space, with its relations. A region can be declared by the pattern, by knowledge or by the user (mechanism 5), which is gap T.

## 3. Every operation on a whole and every reaction is a re-completion under a cost

6.8 already has "completare cu cost minim": free roles, candidates, hard constraints, the cheapest wins. The proposal is that all the short commands are that mechanism, each defined by two things only: which roles it frees in the existing result, and which term it adds to the cost.

| Command | Roles freed | Cost term added | Gap |
|---|---|---|---|
| arrange better / more naturally / logically | the places | the soft issues about place, overlaps, crowding | F |
| nicer, beautiful | places and the qualities the system chose | the declared objective of that kind of whole | F |
| as a hexagon, in a row, in the form of a house | the places | distance of each member to a position of the pattern | M |
| something else, another one, redo | everything the system chose | far from the previous result | E |
| you overdid it, too much | the dimension farthest above the usual | distance to the usual | Q |
| draw something, whatever you want, random | the kind and the structure | distance to the usual, with variety | J |
| fewer, more, bigger, later | one role | a step in one direction | (already in the book) |

So the book needs one definition (re-completion: what is kept, what is freed, what is added to the cost, how the result is explained from the terms that changed) and one table, instead of an operation per phrase. "Keep X" (15-11 #33) is the same seen from the other side: a role that may not be freed.

## 4. The journal is a world: talking about what happened uses the same acts and references

6.11 made the journal an instance: requests, steps, replies and observations are facts. Then the last step is a thing, and a message about the conversation is an ordinary request whose object is in the journal.

| Message | The same thing in general terms | Gap |
|---|---|---|
| undo | remove the last step | (in the book) |
| wrong; no, not that; I said X | remove the last step and redo its request with one role corrected | D, K |
| why? how come? | a question about a step, answered from its facts | D, 6.11 |
| the lake is not there | a statement about the state that contradicts a reply: read again, then correct the state or the claim | D |
| put it back where it was | a past value read from a step (mechanism 1, row 3) | O |
| where is Paris? | a question about knowledge, answered from its facts with the source | P |

A message is then one of three things: an act on a domain's world, an act or question on the journal, a question on knowledge. The table of message kinds in 6.4 becomes that 3-way split, with fragments and reactions as the short forms of the first two.

## 5. The user teaches the instance, with the same kinds of fact that knowledge has

The book has one case: degree factors learnt per user (4.5.1). The same mechanism — a fact in the instance, with the user as source, nearer than shared knowledge and therefore winning (R41), undone with its step, never promoted by itself — covers:

| What the user teaches | Fact of the same kind as | Gap |
|---|---|---|
| a name for a thing ("the lake") | a name | B |
| named regions ("three levels: water, land, sky") | a region of a space | T |
| what is usual ("a diagram has 5–10 nodes and arrows") | a usual value | U |
| how much "big" is | a degree factor | (in the book) |

## 6. What is left over

Two things do not reduce. A topic with structure (V): but it is a description of the same form as a request or a reference (kind, qualities, relations), so the book needs ONE definition of "description" used by all three. And the fallback of drawing by composition from basic shapes (the end of G): a real capability the drawing tool must have; the rule about it is mechanism 1, the capability is program.

So: five mechanisms and one shared structure (the description), instead of 22. The test I would apply to this reduction, and ask the author to apply: each of the 106 owner turns must still derive from the five, and no row of the tables above may need a sentence that mentions its own example.
